Mercy Presbyterian Church — New Website Requirements

What the new mercydallas.com should do.

Prepared by Nathan Murray · Based on a review of the current site at mercydallas.com

What this is. A plain-English list of everything the church website needs to do. Not a design, not a tech plan — just the capabilities. Once we agree on the list, we can decide how to build it (rebuild on the current platform, move to a new one, or hire someone).

How to read it. Each item describes one capability. Tags show whether we have it today on the current site (Have today), or it's currently weak or missing (Gap). Bring your edits, additions, or "we don't need this" notes to the next meeting.

1. Who We Are

Anyone landing on the site — a visitor, a curious neighbor, a family relocating to Dallas — should quickly understand what kind of church Mercy is and how to come.

Welcome & what to expect on Sunday Have today
A page that introduces Mercy to a first-time visitor: service time, what worship looks like (expository preaching, weekly communion, music, liturgy), PCA affiliation, where to park, what to do with kids.
Currently covered on /welcome/. The new site should expand parking and "what to expect" detail.
Mission & commitments Have today
A statement of who we are and our three commitments (Gathering, Growing, Going).
Beliefs / "What we believe" Gap
A short, plain-language statement of doctrine for newcomers, with a link to the Westminster Standards / PCA's Book of Church Order for those who want depth.
Currently implied through PCA affiliation but not stated directly.
Leadership directory Have today
Photos and bios for staff, pastor, elders, and deacons. Each entry should include the person's role, and a way to contact them (or the group) by email.
Currently lists 9 elders, 6 deacons, 6 staff with email links. Photos are uneven — the new site should standardize portraits.
Location, directions & service times Gap
An interactive map with the address, a note that we meet inside Temple Shalom, parking instructions, accessibility info, and Sunday service time prominently displayed.
Today the /map/ page shows the address as text only. There is no embedded map, no parking diagram, and no accessibility notes.
Contact & phone number Have today
Address, phone, and a general email visible on every page.

2. Sermons & Worship

This is the most-used part of the current site. Visitors find us through sermons; members re-listen and share them.

Sermon archive Have today
A searchable, filterable library of every sermon preached at Mercy. Filter by preacher, sermon series, topic, and book of the Bible. Sort by date.
Today: ~418 sermons across 36 series. Filters work. Pagination is heavy (32 pages) — the new site should have better search.
Individual sermon pages Have today
Each sermon has a page with: title, date, preacher, series, scripture passage, an audio player, and a download link.
Podcast feed Gap
Sermons should be available as a podcast people can subscribe to in Apple Podcasts, Spotify, etc. — not just played on the website.
No public podcast feed surfaced today.
Sermon notes & manuscripts Gap
Optional: attach a PDF of the sermon manuscript or outline to each sermon for those who like to read along or revisit.
Not present today. Decision needed: do we want this?
Live stream Have today
Sunday service at 10 AM streamed live for members who can't attend. Past services available for replay.
Today the "Watch Live" page links out to YouTube (channel UCBS8B-OvEckPZ60nguz7hHA). Consider embedding it on-site so people don't leave.
Order of worship Have today
A downloadable bulletin / order of worship for the current Sunday, with hymns, scripture readings, and prayers.
Today: PDF on the Watch Live page. Consider an HTML version so it's readable on a phone without downloading.

3. Ministries & Community Life

Ministry pages (Women's, Men's, Children's, Youth) Have today
Each ministry has its own page describing what it does, who it's for, meeting times, the leader, and how to get involved or register.
Children's ministry should keep its age-group breakdown (nursery / toddler SS / Children's Church / Elementary SS) and the parenting resources PDF.
Community groups directory Gap
A list of every community group with: the host family, neighborhood/zip code, meeting night, life stage focus, and how to join. Today this exists only as a single descriptive paragraph — new members have to email to learn anything specific.
Events calendar Gap
A real calendar — not a stub page — showing upcoming services, studies, retreats, fellowship events, work days, and meetings. Filterable by ministry. Events should have detail pages with location, description, and a "register" or "RSVP" button.
Today /calendar/ runs on the All-in-One Event Calendar plugin but the public view is sparse. The new site should make the calendar a first-class feature.
Event registration Have today
For studies, retreats, and dinners that require a head count, users should be able to register online — including listing dietary needs, childcare needs, or paying a fee where applicable.
Today: handled in Church Center (Planning Center). The new website should link cleanly to those registration forms.
Bible study materials Gap
Current Bible study books, dates, and discussion guides easily findable in one place — not spread across each ministry page.

4. Get Involved

Volunteer / Serve at Mercy Have today
List the roles — greeters, hospitality, sound, music, children's ministry, communion table guild — and let people sign up directly, not just email and wait.
Today the page describes the roles but offers no signup form. New site should have a real form.
Serve in Dallas (mission partners) Have today
Showcase our local and global mission partners (currently RUF, Casa Del Lago, RUF International), with a paragraph on each and a way to give or volunteer.
Newsletter signup Gap
A real email signup form on the site — one click, name and email, done.
Today the "subscribe" link just opens a mailto with a pre-filled subject line. Most visitors will not complete that.
Contact form Gap
A short "ask a question / I'm new / get in touch" form that routes to the right staff member.
Today everything is a mailto link, which doesn't work well from many devices.
Careers / open positions Have today
A page that shows current openings or says we have none. Optional: a way to express interest for future roles.

5. Giving

Online giving Have today
One-time and recurring gifts, multiple funds (general, missions, special), gift confirmation by email, year-end giving statements.
Today: handled in Church Center at mercydallas.churchcenter.com/giving. The new site should keep this integration — or replace it consciously, not by accident.
Why we give Gap
A short page that frames giving theologically and answers practical questions (how is it used, is it tax-deductible, who can I ask).

6. Member Resources

A members-only area is needed for content that isn't appropriate to expose publicly.

Member login Have today
Members sign in with the email the church has on file to access protected content.
Today this is handled by Instant Church Directory's separate app + portal. A more unified experience would be cleaner.
Pictorial directory Have today
A photo directory of members and regular attendees, accessible by phone app or web. Members opt-in to share their info.
Forms Have today
Downloadable forms for members: reimbursement, youth release, etc.
New site should consider replacing PDFs with fillable online forms where appropriate.
Policies & parent resources Have today
Children's ministry policies, resources for parents, and similar reference documents.
Prayer requests Gap
A way for members to submit a prayer request that goes to the elders / prayer team.

7. How the Site Works (Cross-Cutting)

These aren't "features" per se — they're qualities the whole site needs.

Mobile-friendly
More than half of visits will be from phones. Every page needs to be readable and navigable on a small screen.
Easy for staff to update
Alison and any future staff should be able to add a sermon, post an event, or update a ministry page without a developer. No more than a 30-minute training session for the basics.
Site search
A search box that covers sermons, events, pages, and people — not just sermons.
Visually consistent & warm
A clear, simple visual identity. Photos of our actual sanctuary, people, and Sundays — not stock images. Reflects "Mercy to us, Mercy through us."
Fast & accessible
Pages load quickly. Text is readable. Works for screen readers and for visitors with limited vision.
Findable in Google
Someone searching "Presbyterian church near Hillcrest Dallas" or "PCA Dallas" should be able to find us. Basic SEO done right.
Social presence linked
Footer links to Instagram (@mercypresdallas) and Facebook. Sermons and events should be easy to share.

Summary: What's the Current Site Doing?

Today's site is a WordPress site on the Divi theme, with two main plugins (Sermon Manager for sermons, All-in-One Event Calendar for events). It integrates with three external services:

Church Center
Giving, event registration
YouTube
Sunday live stream & archive
Instant Church Directory
Pictorial member directory
(No tool today)
Newsletter signup — just a mailto link

The current site does the basics well (sermons, identity, ministry overviews). The main gaps are around the things visitors and members actually do: register, find an event, join a community group, sign up to volunteer, give a prayer request, get the newsletter. Many of those flow through email today, which adds friction.

Questions for Discussion

  1. Are we missing anything? What does the website need to do that isn't on this list?
  2. Are we doing too much? Anything here we should drop or simplify?
  3. Members area: Do we want a unified login, or keep the separate Instant Church Directory app?
  4. Calendar & registration: Should everything (events, registration, giving, directory) live in Church Center / Planning Center? Or do we want the public website to handle some of it directly?
  5. Sermons: Do we want a real podcast feed and on-demand video, not just YouTube links?
  6. Visitor experience: When a first-time visitor lands on the homepage, what's the single most important thing we want them to do or know?
  7. Who owns it after launch? Who keeps it up to date — staff, a volunteer, an outside vendor?
